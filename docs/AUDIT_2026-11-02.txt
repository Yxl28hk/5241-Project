- Prevent duplicate login submissions and allow retry after a failed request.
- Batch quiz seed writes while retaining existing upsert and teacher-authored question preservation rules.

Remaining product/deployment limitations:
- Teacher registration is self-selected, with no identity approval workflow.
- No verified-email or forgotten-password delivery workflow.
- Password changes do not revoke every other existing signed-cookie session.
- Login throttling and AI task queues are process-local; multi-worker deployment needs shared storage.
- Stable SECRET_KEY, HTTPS cookies and reverse-proxy origin handling need deployment configuration.
- Scanned PDFs require OCR which is not implemented.
- This audit does not establish absence of all defects or readiness for public production use.
